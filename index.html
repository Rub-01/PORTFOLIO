A LITTLE CONTEXT

Beyond the checklist.

I learn best by getting hands-on: following evidence, asking better questions, and documenting what I find.

My interest in cybersecurity sits at the intersection of security operations, threat detection, and digital forensics. I enjoy the investigative process: looking at an unfamiliar signal, gathering context, testing hypotheses, and explaining the result clearly.

Through practical labs and small projects, I'm building a stronger understanding of Windows telemetry, SIEM workflows, suspicious email analysis, and the ways Python can reduce repetitive investigation work.

This hub is a working record of that process—not just what I have studied, but the experiments, evidence, and lessons I can share along the way.
